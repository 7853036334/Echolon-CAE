WHAT WE DO

CAE services for child restraint systems

Engineering analysis services covering the structure, occupant safety and materials of your baby car seat.

Structural Analysis

Static and fatigue checks on the shell, base and harness anchors under expected loads.

Imapact Simulation

Crash simulations for frontal, side and rear impact scenarios designed to meet FMVSS 213 performance and injury criteria standards.

Regulatory Compliance (FMVSS 213)

Virtual pre-test validation for dynamic frontal and side-impact performance standards to ensure first-time physical test success.

Ergonomics

Posture, pressure and fit studies using infant and child occupant models.

Material Analysis

Compare PP, ABS and EPP foam behaviour, from stiffness to energy absorption.

Design Optimization

Explore design improvements that reduce weight and cost while meeting engineering targets.

HOW WE WORK

Our engineering process

Each project follows a structured workflow, with engineering results reviewed at every stage.

01

CAD Modeling

Build or prepare the seat geometry using the supplied CAD model.

02

Simulation Setup

Prepare the mesh, materials, loads and contact conditions.

03

Analysis

Review stress, strain, deformation and relevant occupant-safety indicators.

04

Design Improvement

Evaluate changes to ribs, thickness and materials, then rerun simulations.

05

Validation & FMVSS 213 Check

Compare simulation findings with available physical test data and prepare a report.
